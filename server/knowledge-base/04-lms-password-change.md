# Login to LearnOrg and Moodle — first-time password change

> Source: https://uom.lk/cites/support/lms-password-change
> Retrieved: 2026-07-30 · Status: SOURCED (extracted from the live CITeS page — treat the URL as authoritative for anything not covered here)

### Q: How does a staff member change their LMS password for the first time?

1. Go to `lms.uom.lk`.
2. Enter your username and the temporary password sent by SMS to the mobile number given on your request form.
3. Replace the temporary password with a personal one: use the SMS password as your "current password", then create a new password following the policy requirements shown on screen. This new credential also applies to Moodle, WebMail, and related services. Other services stay inaccessible until the password change is completed.

### Q: How does a student change their LMS/Moodle password for the first time?

1. Go to `lms.uom.lk`.
2. Enter your credentials based on your enrolment cohort:
   - Batch 2019 or later: username is your name-with-initials plus your batch, e.g. `edirisinheyd.19`.
   - Batch 2018 or earlier: username is your index number, e.g. `123456D`.
   - The default password is your index number.
3. Change the default password to a personal one: use your index number as the "current password", then create a new password per the policy requirements shown on screen. Once changed, access Moodle through `online.uom.lk`. Other services require the password change to be completed first.
