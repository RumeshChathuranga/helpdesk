# IT Help Desk — who can use it and how to reach CITeS

> Source: https://uom.lk/cites/services/it-help-desk and https://uom.lk/cites
> Retrieved: 2026-07-30 · Status: SOURCED (extracted from the live CITeS pages — treat the URLs as authoritative for anything not covered here)

### Q: Who can use the UoM IT Help Desk, and how do I log in?

The IT Help Desk ticketing portal at `helpdesk.uom.lk` is open to registered users in Academic, Academic Support, Administrative, Technical, and Non-Academic staff, as well as students. Log in with your University email credentials.

### Q: What kind of issues does the IT Help Desk handle?

The published categories are: DMS System issues, DNS requests, Email configuration and issues, Email lists, ERP system matters, IP phones, LearnOrg/MIS modules (Exam, Postgraduate, SRC), and licence requests. The wider CITeS service catalogue also covers eduroam/Wi-Fi, Moodle, Zoom, Office 365, WebMail, VPN, the external proxy, and account provisioning.
