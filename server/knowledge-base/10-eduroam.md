### Q: What is eduroam?

eduroam is the secure, world-wide roaming access service developed for the international research and education community. It operates across more than 80 territories, letting staff and students connect to Wi-Fi at any participating institution using their home institution's credentials. Coverage is built on the 802.1X standard and a hierarchy of RADIUS proxy servers.

### Q: Where can I find eduroam setup and troubleshooting instructions?

The CITeS eduroam page is mainly a hub with links to the official eduroam website and the LEARN Eduroam Sri Lanka portal for detailed connection instructions, security notes, and coverage information — it does not itself list step-by-step setup instructions per device.
