# University of Moratuwa IT Policy

> Source: https://uom.lk/policies/it-policy
> Retrieved: 2026-07-30 · Status: SOURCED (extracted from the live CITeS page — treat the URL as authoritative for anything not covered here)

### Q: Where can I find the University of Moratuwa IT Policy?

The IT Policy is published as a PDF ("UoM ICT Policy.pdf") on the University of Moratuwa policies page at `uom.lk/policies/it-policy`. The web page itself is only a download link — it does not display the policy's sections or requirements inline, so its substantive content is not reproduced here.

### Q: Can this help desk interpret or make an exception to the IT Policy?

No. Any request that asks for an interpretation of, or an exception to, the University of Moratuwa IT Policy or another university regulation must be escalated to a human — this is not something the automated assistant can decide or state a position on. Direct the requester to the PDF above and escalate the ticket.
