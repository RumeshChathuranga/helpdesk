# Reset your UoM account password

> Source: https://uom.lk/cites/support/reset-your-uom-account-password
> Retrieved: 2026-07-30 · Status: SOURCED (extracted from the live CITeS page — treat the URL as authoritative for anything not covered here)

### Q: How do I reset my UoM account password?

1. Click on **Forgot my password**.
2. Read the instructions first, and then enter the requested details in the given text boxes. Then press **Send OTP**.
3. Wait for the OTP. An OTP will be sent to your secondary email and to your mobile number.
4. Set a new password for your account. Make sure to read the password policy first.

If you do not receive the OTP after a reasonable wait, check that the secondary email and mobile number on your account are correct, and contact the CITeS IT Help Desk if the problem continues.

### Q: Where is the UoM password policy?

See the User Accounts and Password Policy page: `/cites/policy/password-policy` on uom.lk. Read it before setting a new password — the reset form will reject a password that does not meet it.
